## HOW TO USE THESE SEQUENCES:

### For Single Analysis:
1. Copy any sequence above (without the ## comments)
2. Paste into the DNA Analysis tab
3. Click "Analyze DNA Sample"

### For Comparison:
1. Copy Sample A (Sequence 6) into "Sequence 1"
2. Copy Sample B (Sequence 7) into "Sequence 2"
3. Click "Compare DNA Sequences"
4. Expected Result: ~95% similarity (high match)

### For Batch Processing:
1. Save each sequence as separate .txt or .fasta files
2. Upload multiple files at once
3. Click "Process All Files"

- Sequence 1: RELIABLE (quality_score > 0.9)
- Sequence 2: ACCEPTABLE (quality_score ~0.7)
- Sequence 3: UNRELIABLE (too short)
